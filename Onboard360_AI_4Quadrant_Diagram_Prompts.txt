Onboard360 AI — 4-Quadrant Diagram Prompts
==================================================

1️⃣ Quadrant 1 — Current Challenges
-----------------------------------
Create a professional enterprise infographic titled “Current Challenges in Partner Onboarding.”
Visualize multiple disconnected icons such as Excel sheets, email envelopes, Jira tickets, Kafka topics, and folders, all scattered and loosely connected with broken or dashed lines.

Use gray and red color tones to represent inefficiency, manual work, and chaos.
Add subtle text labels near clusters:
- “Manual SME Validation”
- “Fragmented Tools”
- “Delayed Turnaround”
- “No Central Visibility”

Background: light gray to white gradient.
Style: clean, flat infographic suitable for leadership slides.
Typography: Segoe UI / Open Sans.
Add a faint red warning triangle icon ⚠️ in a corner for emphasis.


2️⃣ Quadrant 2 — Why We Must Transform
--------------------------------------
Create a transformation-focused infographic titled “Why We Must Transform Partner Onboarding.”
Visualize multiple disconnected systems (Excel, Jira, APIGEE, Kafka, IAM) converging into a single unified center hub labeled “Onboard360 AI.”

Use flowing arrows to represent data integration and orchestration.
Add key callouts such as:
- “Disjointed Systems → Unified Platform”
- “Manual Review → Automated Validation”
- “Limited Pickup (<10%) → Scalable Adoption”

Color theme: maroon → purple gradient showing movement toward modernization.
Use subtle animation-like flow lines or glows to depict evolution.
Style: minimalist enterprise infographic with futuristic orchestration feel.


3️⃣ Quadrant 3 — Strategic Impact Areas
---------------------------------------
Design a modern business impact diagram titled “Strategic Impact Areas.”

Divide the diagram into four segments with icons and concise captions:
- 🎯 Product Management: Accelerated launches, reduced rework.
- 👥 Leadership: SLA visibility and governance dashboards.
- 📊 Partner Resources: SME empowerment, workload balance.
- 💰 Financial: Efficiency-driven cost optimization.

Represent these as a circular or diamond-shaped layout around a subtle central hub labeled “Onboard360 AI Impact.”

Use green, blue, and purple accent tones with light white background.
Keep icons line-based, clean, and consistent.
Executive-grade style suitable for PowerPoint integration.


4️⃣ Quadrant 4 — The Vision — Onboard360 AI
--------------------------------------------
Create a centerpiece visual titled “The Vision — Onboard360 AI.”
Show an AI Copilot brain icon or glowing orb at the center with connected radial branches labeled:
- “Automation”
- “Validation”
- “Guidance”
- “Governance”
- “SLA Tracking”
- “Compliance Intelligence”

Each branch should have soft curved lines leading to small, meaningful icons (gears, check marks, chat bubbles, lock, graph).

Central glow color: purple #6F3198; accent highlights: green #009933 and maroon #7F0000.

Background: minimal white or faint gradient; overall tone — intelligent, futuristic, and calm.
Caption at bottom: “AI acts as a co‑pilot, not a replacement — enabling intelligent onboarding.”
