Onboard360 AI – Diagram Generation Prompts
==================================================

1️⃣ 4‑Quadrant Infographic — “Why Transform Partner Onboarding”
---------------------------------------------------------------
Create a professional 4‑quadrant infographic titled “Why Partner Onboarding Must Transform”.
Each quadrant should have clean icons and minimal text captions with soft gradients.

Quadrants:
- Top Left (Current Challenges): Show fragmented icons like Excel, Email, Kafka, and Jira in disconnected circles — theme: “Manual, Fragmented, Inefficient”.
- Top Right (Transformation Need): Flow arrows showing integration of multiple systems into a unified center. Theme: “Disconnected → Orchestrated”.
- Bottom Left (Strategic Impact): Show measurable outcomes — dashboard visuals, SLA indicators, and leadership analytics.
- Bottom Right (Vision – Onboard360 AI): Centered AI brain or copilot icon with automation, validation, guidance, and governance callouts.

Use enterprise‑grade design (white background, deep maroon #7F0000, purple #6F3198, accent green #009933).
Typography: Segoe UI / Open Sans, flat 2D look, minimalistic executive tone.


2️⃣ Before → After Diagram — “From Manual Chaos to Unified Intelligence”
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Design a horizontal Before → After process transformation visual.
Left side shows chaos — messy flow of Excel sheets, emails, Kafka topics, and Jira tickets scattered around. Use muted gray tones.
Right side shows Onboard360 AI hub connecting clean, organized icons for Intake, IAM, Metadata, SLA, and Governance — glowing purple/green lines to show orchestration.

In the center, add a transformation arrow labeled “AI‑Driven Automation & Contextual Intelligence”.

Caption below: “Transforming fragmented manual onboarding into a unified, intelligent ecosystem.”

Keep modern enterprise look, with soft shadows, gradient backgrounds, and a white canvas.

Style: minimalist / enterprise infographic, perfect for PowerPoint insertion.

Each stage should have a clean icon and a connecting arrow.

Keep the background minimal, professional, and readable when embedded in PPT.
